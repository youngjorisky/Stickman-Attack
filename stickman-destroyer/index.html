A Brief History of Typography

Long before screens, people carved letters into stone and hoped nobody would argue with them. Printing presses made words cheap, and cheap words multiplied until every wall, menu, and cereal box had something to say.

Today every page is a crowd of tiny glyphs standing very still. They trust that the cursor is the only thing that will ever bother them, and that trust is about to be tested by a determined little stick figure.
